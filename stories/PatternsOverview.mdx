# Patterns

A **pattern** is a preset combination of existing components for a recurring UI need — a
whole interaction, not a single element. Take the *Delete item modal*: it's just `NeModal`
pre-wired with a confirmation input, danger styling and the right labels, so you don't have
to rebuild it from scratch every time.

## Pattern or component?

- Reach for a **pattern** when you want the complete, opinionated solution and consistent
  behavior across the product.
- Drop down to the underlying **components** when your case is a bit different and you need
  full control over how things fit together.

Have a browse through the sidebar — each pattern comes with a live example.

The texts of each pattern follow the
<a href="https://nethesis.github.io/design-system/?page=writing-guidelines" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">writing guidelines</a>
of the Nethesis Design System: the description of the pattern links its section. When you
change them, change the guidelines too, or the other way round.
